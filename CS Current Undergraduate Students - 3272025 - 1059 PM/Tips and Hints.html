Problem: How do I know what is wrong with my program after I submit it?

Solution: once you login on the UVA, click "My Submissions" on the left menu. You will see the status of your submission. A "Compilation error" would have more details if you click on it. But a "Wrong Answer" is not something you can get more info on. One tip is to read the question again carefully and look for any assumptions you may have missed in the input. Also, you can go to the UVAtoolkit.com site and try sample input to see what the output should be, or create more test cases.

Problem: Can I find some good problem solution sets to get started?

Solution: there are many solutions that people post online. However, a good set is from past competitions: go to http://acm-ecna.ysu.edu/ and look for problem sets and past results. Often you will find solutions in C/C++ and Java. The Practice problems are much easier for you to start with than the real contest problems.

Problem: Which problems I should start solving?

Solution: From the UVA problem set, go to "AOAPC I: Beginning Algorithm Contests (Rujia Liu)" start with the "getting started" problems.

Problem: I don't seem to be able to solve anything. Even when I try I keep getting Wrong Answer even when I know it is right. Should I drop this course?

Solution: It is common to have some solutions not judged correctly, however you will also receive part marks for attempts. My advice, if you are serious about programming, don't get frustrated, just tough it out and go to the next problem. It takes a lot of patience and practice to become a competitive programmer. Bring your question to class or use the discussion forum in this site to get help from your peers. Two heads better than one! It is after all a team contest to begin with.
