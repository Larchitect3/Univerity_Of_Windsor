The ACM (Associate for Computing Machinery) with IBM sponsors an annual international collegiate programming competition (ICPC). The contest is held at a regional level based on the geographic area, and the winners advance to compete at the international level. We are in the East Central North American (ECNA) region.

You can find useful information that you should read here: https://icpc.baylor.edu/

Specifically read the following: 

Regional Rules: https://icpc.baylor.edu/regionals/rules

Preparation: https://icpc.baylor.edu/compete/preparation

Problems: https://icpc.baylor.edu/compete/problems
