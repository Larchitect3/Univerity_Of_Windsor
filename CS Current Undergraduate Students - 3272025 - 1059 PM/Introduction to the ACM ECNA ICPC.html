Welcome!

First off, the "ACM" stands for the Association for Computing Machinery and is a world elite organization where all Computer Science students and professionals converge to find the latest information, conferences, and journals about new research in Computer Science. 

That said, the ACM organizes an annual ICPC, or International Computer Programming Competition, sponsored by IBM.

Before participating in the International competition, a team has to win the Regional competition. Our university is located in ECNA, or the East Central North American region. The regional contests represents the geographic area of Ohio, Michigan, eastern Ontario (all the corridor from Windsor to Toronto to Ottawa), western Pennsylvania, and Indiana (excluding the Greater Chicago Metropolitan area).

Before participating in the regional competition, a team has to win the Local competition. Our university holds a local competition in early October and often the top three teams qualify to participate in the Regional competition. 

Why participate? 

- you can participate as a contestant or as a volunteer (we are always in need for volunteers to help us organize the regional contest which we host at the end of October).

- the experience is valuable and you can put it on your resume as a professional activity. This will help you in your job search.

- working the problems will help you sharpen your programming skills as you work your way to be one of the elites.

- lots of free food and prizes, it is a lot of fun to meet like minded colleagues.
