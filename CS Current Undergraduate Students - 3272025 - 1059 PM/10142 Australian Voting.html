This is from the Getting Started Section of the textbook. 

https://uva.onlinejudge.org/index.php?option=com_onlinejudge&Itemid=8&category=29&page=show_problem&problem=1083

The hints here are:

1. Make sure you understand the question requirements, not what you think they meant rather, what they actually meant. 

2. Make use of the UDebug and UVAToolkit sites to help you get started in debugging and troubleshooting valid cases and create more sample data.

3. Watch out for special cases! (e.g. ties)

4. Create a good test case(s) sample. Don't just use the sample given.

5. First use a pen and paper to explain how they got the output, then make more cases, then come up with a good algorithm.... then you implement

6. implement step by step, case by case, (do the input/output first) then work your way by passing each test case.
