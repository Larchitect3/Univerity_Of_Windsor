The UVA Online Judge: https://uva.onlinejudge.org/

This site contains thousands of problems, each assigned a unique UVA ID. 

IMPORTANT TASK: Create a user account on this website. It is free and it allows you to submit solutions to any problem on the site that can be automatically judged. It maintains your statistics which you will need to show me at the end of this course.

There are several companion sites: UHunt, UVA Toolkit, and other online solutions and discussion boards.

CAUTION: Never submit some else's solution as your own! You are to uphold a code of honour in this course and submit your own work. If you fail to do so you will be charged with academic misconduct as per senate bylaw 31. You will be asked to submit all your code that you are counting for your marks at the end of the course and I will check them.

Your goal in this course is to pick as many problems as you can handle and solve them. Submit your solutions to the automated judge on the UVA website and refer to your course syllabus for the grading system. Start this immediately so you can solve at least 1 to 2 problems a week.
