These strict duties apply on Saturday during the regional contest (we are more flexible on Friday as we get the kinks out and practice)

Proctor: 1) Ensure only students with contest badges/shirts assigned to your lab may enter the lab. Not Badge/Shirt No Entry No Food. 2) Ensure no electronics/cell-phones no calculators in the lab (instruct them to leave their electronics with their coaches or they are disqualified). 3) Teams may not communicate with each other, nor with their coaches. 4) Just like proctoring a test, no cheating allowed. 5) team members may leave for washrooms, may leave for food, may bring food in, must clean-up and avoid spills.6) May not communicate in hallways with other teams or coaches. 7) May not ask you any questions related to the contest problems, instruct them to send a clarification to the judge using the system provided. 8) Report any technical issues or problems directly to the contest director (Dr. Kobti). 9) If a team prints, they can notify you that they printed, the runners will bring the print job to you, or you can let the runners know to bring a print job. Do not leave your post and leave the room unattended. Tag team with your partner proctor should you need to leave for a short break ensuring there is at least one proctor in the room at all times. 10) you are responsible for opening/closing/locking the room behind ensuring all team belongings are out of the room when the contest is done and the right people are in the room when the contest starts. 11) You are responsible for assisting in distributing the contest questions at the start of the contest, one booklet for each contestant, with minimal delay in the distribution. It is a very important and stressful step to ensure all teams start at the same time just when the clock starts. Direct the coaches to go to the coaches room as they are not allowed to be in the lab or mingling with their students during the official contest on Saturday.

Runner: You are stationed next to the printer in the Technical lounge. Familiarize yourself with the team seating arrangements and be responsible for delivering print-jobs as they come to the right team. At the start of the contest you will assist in the general seating, guiding the teams to their locations, and filling any shortage of proctors if necessary.You may also substitute for Hallway Monitor when you see the need or when the food is out (lunch) and teams are lining up in the hallway to get their food. Ensure no communication or trading competition answers/discussions may occur.

Hallway Monitor: No Shirt/No Name Tag = no access to food or labs, ask them politely to leave the contest area. Report any issues to the contest director (Dr. Kobti). Fill in for Proctor if required. Guide people to their respective labs. Familiarize yourself with the team seating arrangements. At the start of the contest assist in the distribution of contests.You are a second Runner if there is no food and no people in the halls. Coaches must stay in the coaches room.

Registration Desk: This occurs on Friday afternoon when the teams are arriving. Very important to be organized and welcoming to our clients. Professional behavior is imperative in front of our visitors at all times. Ensure each team COACH is present to sign-in his/her team(s) checking them off your list and providing them with their package which includes: Instructions, name badges, T-Shirts, Certificates, Swag. At the end of the registration period you bring in all the extra materials to the technical area. Volunteers do not receive or give-away swag. All volunteers must be wearing their proper contest shirts (special colors for volunteers, coaches and contestants). Very important to provide the correct information to our visitors so please familiarize yourself with the schedule and nearby food locations/parking (best in parking garage to avoid tickets). You must make sure the registration area is cleaned up before the contest starts.
